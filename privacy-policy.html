Privacy Policy

Ghostep: Ghost Jump Platformer, by Exalted Lion

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how Exalted Lion ("we", "us") handles information in the game Ghostep: Ghost Jump Platformer (the "Game"), published on Google Play as com.exaltedlion.ghostep.

In short: the Game doesn't collect, send or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Information we collect

None. The Game doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It doesn't send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Information saved on your device

The Game saves your progress (levels cleared, stars and deaths) and your settings (music, sound, vibration and effects) on your device so you can carry on where you left off. This information stays on your device and we can't see it. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in your device's settings, deletes it.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google Account, Android may include this saved data in your device backup. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and we can't access it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: lets the Game vibrate your device when something happens in play. You can turn it off in the Game's Settings.
  • Network access: part of the game engine the Game is built with. The Game doesn't use it to send or receive information about you. Tapping Privacy Policy in Settings opens this page in your web browser.

Third-party services

The Game is made with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics, crash reporting, advertising and other online services are turned off in the Game, so Unity receives no data from it. The Game contains no advertising or analytics software.

You download the Game from Google Play, which is run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Google gives developers anonymous, combined statistics such as install counts, and crash reports from devices whose owners chose to share them. These don't identify you.

Children

The Game is meant for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal information, we don't sell or share it, and we hold nothing about you to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game handles information differently, for example by adding advertising, we'll update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
