Privacy Policy

Ghostep: Ghost Jump Platformer, by Exalted Lion

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Exalted Lion ("we", "us") handles information in the game Ghostep: Ghost Jump Platformer (the "Game"), published on Google Play as com.exaltedlion.ghostep.

In short: the Game doesn't ask for your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It connects to the internet to get extra worlds, skins and themes, and when it does, our hosting provider, Cloudflare, receives your device's IP address. We don't use it to identify or track you.

Information we don't ask for

The Game doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details. It doesn't use an advertising ID, and it doesn't send your progress or settings to us.

Downloads and internet use

Some worlds, skins and themes are downloaded after you install the Game. It connects to the internet:

  • when it starts, to check for new or updated content;
  • when you download a world, or use a skin or theme that isn't on your device yet;
  • while you play on Wi-Fi, to fetch the next world before you reach it.

These requests go to Cloudflare, the company that hosts this content for us. As with any internet connection, each request shows Cloudflare your device's IP address, the time, the file requested and basic technical details about the software making the request. The Game doesn't add your name, an account, an advertising ID, a device ID or your game progress.

Cloudflare needs this information to deliver the content and keep the service working and secure. We don't keep our own record of these requests, and we don't use them to identify you, track you or show you ads. Cloudflare handles this information under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and may process it in countries other than your own.

You can play without an internet connection. Everything included with the Game and anything you've already downloaded keeps working offline. Turning off your device's connection stops the Game from going online.

Information saved on your device

The Game saves your progress (levels cleared, stars and deaths), your settings (music, sound, vibration and effects), your chosen skin and theme, what you've unlocked and the content you've downloaded on your device, so you can carry on where you left off. We can't see this information. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in your device's settings, deletes it.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google Account, Android may include this saved data in your device backup. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and we can't access it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: lets the Game vibrate your device when something happens in play. You can turn it off in the Game's Settings.
  • Internet access: lets the Game check for and download content, as described above. Tapping Privacy Policy in Settings opens this page in your web browser.
  • Network state: lets the Game see whether you're online and on Wi-Fi, so it only fetches the next world by itself on Wi-Fi.

Third-party services

We use Cloudflare to host the Game's downloadable content, as described above.

The Game is made with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics, crash reporting, advertising and other online services are turned off in the Game, so Unity receives no data from it. The Game contains no advertising or analytics software.

You download the Game from Google Play, which is run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Google gives developers anonymous, combined statistics such as install counts, and crash reports from devices whose owners chose to share them. These don't identify you.

Children

The Game is meant for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We have no account or profile for you, so we usually hold nothing about you to access, correct or delete. Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we process IP addresses for downloads based on our legitimate interest in delivering the Game's content and keeping it secure, and you have the right to object. To ask about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game handles information differently, for example by adding advertising or in-app purchases, we'll update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
